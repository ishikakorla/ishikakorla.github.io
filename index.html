About me

Product designer on Cornell Digital Tech & Innovation, working on CUAPTS, an off-campus housing discovery platform for Cornell students.

Growing up in the Bay Area surrounded by a heavy tech influence, I came to Cornell wanting to explore Cognitive Science and its intersection with tech.

My interest in Cognitive Science arose from an initial curiosity in neurological disorders, as I couldn’t help but wonder when terms such as “autistic” and “Down syndrome” were stripped of their meanings as a diagnosis and were instead weaponized as insults. Hoping to educate others and reduce stigma, I published and presented a paper comparing treatment methods for children with autism and interned at Prodigy Psychiatric Clinic.

While I am now double majoring in both Cognitive Science and Information Science, I find my dedication to human experience and behavior to be something I carry with me. As a product designer, I hope to use my understanding of cognitive diversity to ensure the apps I build are accessible, making the daily routines of every type of person smoother.

Whether I am working on wireframes or redesigning a feature to be more intuitive, I aim to create empathetic digital experiences while challenging the concept of a standard user.
